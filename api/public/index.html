Check it from the car

A shortcut that sends both bridges as a notification the moment your phone connects to the car.

Get the shortcut
  1. Open this page on your iPhone, tap Get the shortcut, and add it.
  2. In Shortcuts, go to Automation, tap +, choose Bluetooth, and pick your car.
  3. Choose Is Connected, set it to Run Immediately, and select Is the Bridge Up.
  4. Or just ask: "Hey Siri, is the bridge up?"
